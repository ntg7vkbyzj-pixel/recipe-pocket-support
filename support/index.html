Support

Recipe Pocket helps you save, organize, shop for, and cook recipes discovered online.

Save a recipe

Open a recipe in a browser or another app, tap the system Share button, and choose Recipe Pocket. The app saves the source link and attempts to extract the recipe title, image, ingredients, and directions.

Recipe details are missing

Some websites do not publish structured recipe information or block webpage requests. Open the saved recipe, tap Edit, and add or correct any missing details.

Recipe Pocket is missing from Share

Open the Share menu, scroll to the end of the app row, choose More, and enable Recipe Pocket. If it still does not appear, restart the device.

iCloud synchronization

Make sure you are signed into iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and Recipe Pocket is allowed to use iCloud. Open Recipe Pocket’s Sync tab and tap Sync Now.

Grocery list

Open a recipe and select Add Ingredients to Grocery List. Grocery items are grouped by aisle, and identical ingredient lines are combined into quantities.

Cooking Mode

Open a recipe and tap the frying-pan icon. Cooking Mode keeps the screen awake and lets you mark ingredients and directions as completed.
